# Recommendation Guidelines & Scientific Framework

Read this when designing or revising workout splits, diet targets, or supplement protocols (before creating or updating `plan.md`).

---

## 1. Prime Directive: Evidence-Tied Prescriptions

- **Traceability**: Every exercise, macro target, and supplement must link directly to verified user data (goal, experience, blood markers, lifestyle baseline, equipment access).
- **No Guessing**: If key parameters are unverified (e.g., training experience, dietary allergies, schedule limits), ask targeted questions before drafting the plan.
- **Autoregulation**: A plan is a live feedback system. Adjust based on empirical performance logs and scale trends, never on rigid assumptions.

---

## 2. Workout Architecture & Periodization

### A. Volume Landmarks (Per Muscle Group Per Week)
Design splits around validated scientific volume landmarks:

| Landmark | Target Weekly Direct Sets | Strategic Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **MV (Maintenance Volume)** | 4 – 6 sets | Preserves current muscle mass during deep cuts or low-frequency phases. |
| **MEV (Minimum Effective Volume)** | 8 – 10 sets | The lowest volume that stimulates measurable hypertrophy or strength gains. |
| **MAV (Maximum Adaptive Volume)** | 12 – 18 sets | The optimal sweet spot where the vast majority of gains occur. |
| **MRV (Maximum Recoverable Volume)** | 20 – 25+ sets | Upper recovery ceiling; exceeding this causes systemic fatigue and overtraining. |

- **Frequency**: Hit each major muscle group **2 to 3 times per week** to maximize the 24–48 hour muscle protein synthesis (MPS) window.
- **Set Volume per Session**: Cap direct working sets per muscle group at **6–10 sets per workout** to avoid "junk volume" (sets performed under high intra-workout fatigue that provide minimal stimulus).

### B. Intensity & Proximity to Failure (RPE / RIR)
Never write static rep schemes without an intensity target:

| RPE | RIR (Reps in Reserve) | Practical Meaning | Application |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **10** | **0 RIR** | Absolute failure; zero reps left with technical form. | Last set of machine/isolation exercises only. |
| **9** | **1 RIR** | 1 rep remaining in the tank. | Primary strength sets, hypertrophy accessory work. |
| **8** | **2 RIR** | 2 reps remaining; heavy working set. | Standard compound work (Squats, Bench, Deadlifts). |
| **7** | **3 RIR** | 3 reps remaining; moderate effort, high bar speed. | Technique work, warm-up ramp, speed work. |

- *Default Target*: Train compound lifts at **RPE 7.5 – 8.5** (1–3 RIR). Train safe isolation/machine work at **RPE 9 – 10** (0–1 RIR).

### C. Progression Models
1. **Dynamic Double Progression (DDP)** *(Default for accessories & machines)*:
   - Prescribe a rep range (e.g., 3 sets of 10–15 reps).
   - Keep the load identical. When the user hits the top of the rep range (15 reps) for all 3 sets, increase weight by the smallest available increment (e.g., 2.5 kg / 5 lbs) and reset back to the bottom (10 reps).
2. **Linear / Step Progression** *(For novice/intermediate compound lifts)*:
   - Fixed sets and reps (e.g., 3 sets of 5 reps). Add a fixed small weight increment each week while maintaining target RPE.
3. **Stimulus-to-Fatigue Ratio (SFR)**:
   - Prioritize exercises that deliver high mechanical tension with minimal systemic axial fatigue (e.g., Romanian Deadlifts over deficit conventional pulls; Hack Squats or Belt Squats over excessive high-volume barbell back squats when spinal fatigue is high).

### D. Deload Protocol
- **Proactive Deload**: Schedule every **6 to 8 weeks** of continuous progressive overload.
- **Reactive Deload**: Trigger immediately if performance drops across **2 consecutive sessions** in primary lifts, or if sleep/joint irritability spikes.
- **Execution**:
  - Reduce working sets by **50%** (e.g., 4 sets $\rightarrow$ 2 sets).
  - Reduce working load by **10–15%**.
  - Maintain movement patterns at **RPE 6 – 7** to retain neural motor patterns without accumulating fatigue.

## 3. Nutrition & Metabolic Calculations

### A. TDEE & Caloric Allocation
1. **BMR Calculation**:
   - *With Known Body Composition*: Use **Katch-McArdle**:
     $$\text{BMR} = 370 + (21.6 \times \text{Lean Body Mass in kg})$$
   - *Without Body Composition*: Use **Mifflin-St Jeor**:
     $$\text{BMR} = (10 \times \text{weight in kg}) + (6.25 \times \text{height in cm}) - (5 \times \text{age}) + s \quad (s = +5\text{ for men}, -161\text{ for women})$$
2. **Activity Multipliers (NEAT + Exercise)**:
   - Sedentary (desk job, $<5,000$ steps): $\text{BMR} \times 1.2$
   - Lightly Active ($5,000 - 8,000$ steps + 3 workouts/wk): $\text{BMR} \times 1.375$
   - Moderately Active ($8,000 - 12,000$ steps + 4-5 workouts/wk): $\text{BMR} \times 1.55$
   - Highly Active ($>12,000$ steps + daily hard training): $\text{BMR} \times 1.725$

3. **Goal-Specific Energy Targets**:
   - **Lean Hypertrophy (Bulk)**: Surplus of **$+200\text{ to }+350\text{ kcal/day}$** (targeting $+0.25\%\text{ to }+0.5\%$ body weight gain per month for non-beginners).
   - **Moderate Fat Loss (Cut)**: Deficit of **$-300\text{ to }-500\text{ kcal/day}$** (targeting $-0.5\%\text{ to }-1.0\%$ body weight loss per week to spare lean muscle mass).

### B. Macronutrient Breakdown
- **Protein**:
  - *Hypertrophy / Maintenance*: $1.6 - 2.2\text{ g/kg}$ of total body weight ($0.8 - 1.0\text{ g/lb}$).
  - *Hypocaloric Deficit (Fat Loss)*: $2.2 - 2.6\text{ g/kg}$ of Lean Body Mass to protect muscle tissue.
  - Distribute across **3 to 5 meals**, each providing at least $2.5 - 3.0\text{ g}$ of Leucine to trigger MPS.
- **Dietary Fats**:
  - Set at **$0.6 - 1.0\text{ g/kg}$** of total body weight (minimum 20–25% of total caloric intake). Never drop fats below $0.5\text{ g/kg}$ to safeguard steroid hormone synthesis (testosterone, progesterone, estradiol).
- **Carbohydrates**:
  - Allocate all remaining calories to carbohydrates to saturate muscle glycogen stores and fuel high-intensity training.
  - Prioritize peri-workout carbs (60–90 minutes pre-workout and immediately post-workout).

### C. Stall Management & Refeeds
Apply adjustments only when the user reports tracked daily weigh-ins (7-day rolling average) and confirms dietary compliance:

1. **Fat-Loss Stall (Weight flat $\ge 14$ days with confirmed adherence)**:
   - Option A: Reduce daily calories by **8–10%** (typically $150-250\text{ kcal}$, primarily from carbohydrates/fats).
   - Option B: Increase daily NEAT baseline by **$+2,000\text{ steps/day}$**.
   - Present both options to the user to choose their preferred adherence mechanism.
2. **Structured 48-Hour Refeed**:
   - For prolonged cuts ($>8\text{ weeks}$): Raise calories to calculated **maintenance for 2 consecutive days**, shifting 100% of the caloric increase into carbohydrates while keeping protein steady and fats minimal ($<30-40\text{ g}$). Temporarily restores leptin, glycogen, and gym performance.
3. **Muscle-Gain Stall (Weight & lifts flat $\ge 21$ days with confirmed adherence)**:
   - Increase daily intake by **$+100\text{ to }+150\text{ kcal/day}$** (carbohydrates).
4. **Post-Diet Reverse Dieting**:
   - Upon completing a fat-loss cut, raise calories by $+100\text{ to }+150\text{ kcal/week}$ back to new maintenance to restore metabolic rate without rapid rebound fat storage.

## 4. Evidence-Based Supplementation Hierarchy

Categorize all supplement recommendations into strict evidence tiers. **Never recommend Tier C compounds.**

```
[Tier A: Foundational / High Evidence]
  Creatine Monohydrate | Whey/Plant Isolate | Caffeine | Vitamin D3+K2 | Omega-3
         │
[Tier B: Situational / Context-Dependent]
  Citrulline Malate | Beta-Alanine | Electrolytes | Magnesium Glycinate
         │
[Tier C: Ineffective / Unregulated / Prohibited]
  SARMs | Prohormones | Extreme Fat Burners | Testosterone Boosters
```

### Tier A (Gold Standard / Universal Efficacy)
- **Creatine Monohydrate**: $3 - 5\text{ g/day}$ continuously. Enhances phosphocreatine resynthesis, cellular hydration, and power output. Loading phase ($20\text{ g/day}\times 5\text{ days}$) is optional.
- **Protein Powder (Whey or Plant Blend)**: Practical convenience tool to hit daily protein targets.
- **Caffeine (Anhydrous or Coffee)**: $3 - 6\text{ mg/kg}$ taken 30–60 minutes prior to workouts for neuromuscular drive and fatigue suppression. Avoid within 8 hours of bedtime.
- **Vitamin $D_3$ (+ $K_2$)**: $2,000 - 5,000\text{ IU/day}$ only if verified $<40\text{ ng/mL}$ in blood work.
- **Omega-3 (EPA / DHA)**: $1.5 - 2.5\text{ g}$ combined EPA/DHA daily for joint lubrication, triglyceride management, and inflammatory resolution.

### Tier B (Situational / Performance-Specific)
- **Citrulline Malate**: $6 - 8\text{ g}$ (2:1 ratio) taken 45 minutes pre-workout. Upregulates endothelial nitric oxide and muscular hyperemia.
- **Beta-Alanine**: $3.2 - 6.4\text{ g/day}$ (daily saturation). Buffers intramuscular carnosine for anaerobic efforts lasting 60–240 seconds.
- **Magnesium Glycinate**: $200 - 400\text{ mg}$ taken 30–60 minutes before sleep for nervous system relaxation and cramp prevention.
- **Electrolytes (Sodium / Potassium)**: Recommended for athletes training in high heat or exceeding 75+ minutes of heavy perspiration.

### Tier C (Strictly Flagged / Avoid)
- **Do not recommend**: SARMs, anabolic prohormones, dangerous thermogenic fat-burners (e.g., yohimbine overdoses, ephedrine/DNP), or proprietary herbal "test-boosters" lacking peer-reviewed clinical validation.
- Flag any supplement that may interact with medications documented in `profile.md`.
