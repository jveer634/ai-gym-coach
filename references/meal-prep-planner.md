# Meal Prep & Weekly Grocery Planner

Read this when designing or revising the user's diet plan, weekly meal prep, recipes, or Sunday grocery list.

---

## 1. Prime Directive: Preference & Schedule Verification

Before prescribing a meal plan or grocery list, verify the following:
1. **Planning Horizon**: Ask whether the user wants **Daily Flexible Planning** (day-by-day flexible meals) or **Entire Week Meal Prep** (bulk batch cooking).
2. **Menu Strategy (Rotational vs. Fixed)**:
   - **Fixed / Consistent Plan**: The same staple meals Monday through Sunday. Ideal for minimizing cooking time, grocery costs, and decision fatigue.
   - **Rotational / Dynamic Plan**: 2–3 rotating meal variations across the week (e.g., Meal Plan A on Mon/Wed/Fri, Meal Plan B on Tue/Thu/Sat). Ideal for preventing palate fatigue while still enabling bulk batch prep.
3. **Primary Shopping Day**: Verify their market/grocery shopping day (e.g., Sunday morning market run).
4. **Dietary Paradigm & Protein Staples**: Vegetarian, Vegan, Non-Vegetarian, or Pescatarian. Identify preferred high-protein staples (e.g., Paneer, Soya chunks/granules, Tofu, Eggs, Chicken breast, Greek yogurt/Curd, Whey).

## 2. The Sunday Grocery Engine

When the user plans for the week, calculate the exact **7-day batch quantities** based on their daily macro targets. Group items by market sections so the user can navigate the grocery store or local vegetable market efficiently:

```
         │

```

### Raw vs. Cooked Weight Accountability
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Macro Accuracy Rule**: Always specify whether food quantities are measured **RAW** or **COOKED**.
> - Soya chunks absorb $2.5-3\times$ their weight in water when boiled (50g raw $\approx$ 150g cooked).
> - Rice expands roughly $2.5-3\times$ when boiled (100g raw $\approx$ 250-300g cooked).
> - Meat and Paneer lose 15–25% water weight when pan-seared or baked.
> - *Default coaching rule*: Direct the user to weigh staples in their **RAW/UNCOOKED** state on a digital kitchen scale for consistent calorie and macro tracking.

## 3. Recipe Suggestions with Video References

Every recipe recommended in the plan must include:
1. **Target Macros per Serving**: Calories, Protein, Carbohydrates, Fat, and Fiber.
2. **Batch Portions**: Ingredients scaled for single serving and 3-to-4 day batch prep.
3. **Step-by-Step Rapid Prep Method**: Efficient cooking instructions (One-pot, Instant Pot, Air-fryer, or Skillet).
4. **YouTube Reference Video Link**: Provide a direct, clickable reference video or search link so the user can visualize the technique, texture, and preparation flow.

## 4. Batch Cooking & Storage Protocols

To ensure meals stay fresh, safe, and appetizing throughout the week:
- **3-Day Freshness Rule**: Cooked meals stored in airtight glass containers remain optimal for **3 to 4 days** in the refrigerator ($4^\circ\text{C} / 40^\circ\text{F}$).
- **Split Batch Strategy**: For a 7-day week, recommend a **Sunday + Wednesday** mini-prep:
  - *Sunday Afternoon*: Cook meals for Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday lunch.
  - *Wednesday Evening*: Quick 45-minute batch prep for Thursday, Friday, and Saturday.
- **Soya Chunks Pre-Treatment**: To eliminate the bitter/earthy taste of soya chunks:
  1. Boil in water with a pinch of salt for 5 minutes.
  2. Drain and rinse under cold water.
  3. Squeeze out excess water thoroughly before marinating or adding to curries/stir-fries.
- **Paneer Handling**: Keep paneer in fresh water in the fridge if unconsumed, or pan-sear on low oil to prevent rubbery texture when reheated.
